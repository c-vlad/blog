# About

This site is a space to think deeply about audience intelligence and leadership, making sense of data and turning it into actionable insight.

## What I Write About

This site documents my thinking across two connected domains:

**Audience Intelligence**  
How organizations move from *having data* to *having intelligence*: structured understanding that can guide decisions and strategy. I explore this from both a functional perspective (what problems are worth solving, which frameworks clarify options) and a technical perspective (how to implement, organize data, and navigate methodological challenges).

**Leadership**  
Reflections on the shift from individual contributor to managing and guiding complex work, and on the mindset, trade-offs, and frameworks that help teams and projects succeed. Some writing draws on experience leading technical work; other pieces are exploratory: ideas, heuristics, frameworks, or hypotheses that help me organize thinking and remember lessons for future discussions with past me. Not everything here is prescriptive; some content exists primarily to surface insights and clarify thought.

## Why This Site Exists

I write to think.

Explaining ideas clearly enough for others to follow forces me to uncover hidden assumptions, test intuitions, and clarify what is possible with data, decisions, and frameworks.

This site is both a research notebook and a long-term memory.

**Primary audience**: Future me (and people like me: data scientists and product strategists wrestling with similar problems).

**Secondary audience**: Strategic thinkers who want a clearer understanding of the potential and limits of audience intelligence.
